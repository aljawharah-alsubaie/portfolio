Munir
Smart Glasses APP

An integrated AI-powered assistive system combining a Flutter mobile app with BLE smart glasses — giving visually impaired users 8 capabilities through a single bilingual voice interface.

Flutter InsightFace Smart Glasses BLE Firebase GPT-4o Accessibility

Hardware

Designed for Simplicity

Munir pairs a Flutter mobile app with the Omi Glass Dev Kit — a lightweight smart glasses platform built around the ESP32-S3 with an integrated camera, microphone, and Bluetooth 5.0. Connect once and everything runs hands-free.

Client-Server Architecture
