Overview

What was built?

Using a dataset with medical attributes — age, cholesterol levels, blood pressure, heart rate, and diabetes status — this project applied two core data mining techniques: classification to identify individuals at high risk of heart attack, and K-means clustering to group patients with similar health profiles and reveal hidden patterns. The result supports data-driven decision-making for early risk detection.

My Contribution

Applied K-means clustering and classification algorithms, performed data preprocessing, built visualizations to communicate risk distribution, and contributed to the final research report.
