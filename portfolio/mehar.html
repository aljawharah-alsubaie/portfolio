About

What is Mehar?

Mehar solves a real gap: discovering and booking local workshops in Saudi Arabia is scattered and inconvenient. The platform unifies the experience — users browse workshops by category (Art, Cooking, Adventure), filter by date and location, book instantly, and share their experiences with the community. Workshop owners get a dedicated dashboard to manage listings and activity.

My Role

Full-stack development — built the booking system, personalized recommendation survey, review module, community explore feed, and user profile pages. Managed project using Agile sprints with Jira.
