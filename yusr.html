What is Yusr?

Performing Hajj and Umrah is a deeply personal spiritual experience â€” but pilgrims face real challenges: losing count of Tawaf and Sa’i circuits, getting separated from family, and health risks in extreme heat. Yusr solves all of this with a single smart wristband that automatically counts rituals using AI motion sensors, tracks the pilgrim’s real-time location via GPS and beacon technology, monitors vital signs, and provides instant SOS emergency connectivity. A companion mobile app (Arabic, English, Urdu) ties everything together.
