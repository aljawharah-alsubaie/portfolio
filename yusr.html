About

What is Yusr?

Pilgrims performing Hajj and Umrah face real challenges: losing count of Tawaf and Sa’i circuits, getting separated from family, and health risks in extreme heat. Yusr solves all of this with a single smart wristband that automatically counts rituals using AI motion sensors, tracks the pilgrim’s real-time location via GPS and beacon technology, monitors vital signs, and provides instant SOS emergency connectivity. A companion mobile app in Arabic, English, and Urdu ties everything together.

💡

About this project — Yusr was developed as a complete entrepreneurship case study for IT427 — IT Entrepreneurship & Innovation at King Saud University. Our work covered the full business side: market research, competitive analysis, business model, 3-year financial projections, and a live marketing campaign that validated real demand. The wristband design and app interfaces shown here are concept visuals that illustrate the product vision — bringing the idea to life as a credible, investable startup.

Features

Key Features

Automatic Ritual Counting

AI motion sensors count Tawaf and Sa’i circuits automatically — no manual tapping required.

Real-Time GPS & Beacon Location

Dual-layer tracking (GPS outdoors + BLE beacons indoors) keeps pilgrims and families connected with gate map navigation.

Health Monitoring

Continuous heart rate, temperature, and SpO2 monitoring — critical in Makkah’s extreme heat. Alerts trigger if vitals are abnormal.

Ritual Guide

Step-by-step Tawaf and Sa’i guide with prayers for each round — so pilgrims never lose their place through all 7 circuits.

Companion Mobile App

Available in Arabic, English, and Urdu — shows ritual progress, live map, health dashboard, and emergency controls.
