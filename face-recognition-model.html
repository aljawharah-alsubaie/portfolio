Context

Why a Custom Evaluation?

Rather than adopting a pre-existing model without justification, we ran a systematic closed-set evaluation of three architectures on a curated VGGFace2 subset under a single-gallery-image constraint — simulating worst-case enrollment conditions. The winning model then went through a full end-to-end evaluation on the SCface surveillance dataset to validate real-world performance.
